Batch Settings

Batch Settings Guide

From / To: The date range for upcoming fixtures. Defaults to a 1-day interval (up to 14 days max).
Strategy: Determines how candidates are selected and ranked.
  • Lower Risk: Prioritizes safer picks (high prob >75%).
  • Balanced: Balances probability (60-78%) and confidence.
  • Value: Focuses on Expected Value and Edge vs Bookmaker odds.
  • Longshot: Finds high-edge picks that are unlikely but highly profitable.
  • High Confidence: Strictest data quality and high sample size.
Batches: Number of separate accumulators to generate.
Min/Max Picks: How many selections to include per batch.
Min/Max Probability: The global allowed percentage range (0-100) for a pick to qualify. Example: 70%. Custom Market Ranges will override this.
Min Confidence: The model's confidence rating of the fixture.
Min Data Quality: The reliability of the historical data (0-100%).
Min EV: Minimum expected value (e.g., 0.05 means 5% expected profit margin).
Min Edge: Minimum probability edge vs bookmakers (e.g., 0.05).
Ranking: How the final qualified picks are sorted to form the best batch.
